Privacy Policy

Nyasara is a wall and interior products brand operated by Arena Enterprises (“Nyasara”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when you interact with Nyasara through our website, WhatsApp, social media advertisements, enquiry forms, customer support, sales staff, or other business channels.

Last Updated: 24 October 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Nyasara, we may collect:

  • Name.
  • Mobile number.
  • City and state.
  • WhatsApp profile name.
  • Customer type, such as homeowner, dealer, contractor or interior designer.
  • Business or dealer details where relevant.
  • Product interests and project requirements.
  • Wall measurements, quantity requirements, installation requirements and purchase timeline.
  • Messages and enquiries sent to Nyasara.
  • Images or photos you voluntarily send to us, including wall, room, product or design images.
  • Call and follow-up information.
  • Enquiry source, including whether an enquiry originated from a Meta, Facebook or Instagram advertisement.
  • Advertising-related information such as ad identifiers, campaign information or referral information where provided by the platform.
  • Website and technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, cookies, requested pages, network information or similar technical data where applicable.

We do not intentionally collect personal information that is not reasonably required for providing our products, services, support, customer communication, business administration or legal obligations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to product and service enquiries.
  • Understand your requirements and recommend suitable wall panels, interior products, designs or finishes.
  • Provide product pricing and product-related information.
  • Arrange calls with our sales team.
  • Prepare project estimates or quotations through our staff.
  • Provide installation or delivery-related assistance.
  • Send catalogues, samples, product images or relevant business information.
  • Manage customer follow-ups and sales enquiries.
  • Improve our customer service, sales processes, website and technology systems.
  • Understand which advertisements or marketing channels generate customer enquiries.
  • Maintain internal customer relationship management and business records.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, duplicate enquiries or technical issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax, regulatory or contractual requirements.

3. WhatsApp and AI-Assisted Customer Support

Nyasara may use automated and AI-assisted systems to help respond to WhatsApp enquiries, understand customer requirements, organise or classify enquiries, suggest relevant responses, recommend appropriate next actions and assist our sales staff.

These systems may process information contained in your messages, including your city, customer type, product interest, wall dimensions, quantity requirements, project needs, purchase timeline and other information you voluntarily provide.

Where appropriate, an enquiry may be transferred to or reviewed by a human Nyasara representative.

AI-assisted systems are not intended to provide legally binding quotations, approve payments, make final contractual commitments or confirm final project costs. Final pricing, product availability, measurements, project specifications and quotations may require confirmation by Nyasara staff.

4. Photos, Images and Visual Information

If you voluntarily send Nyasara a photograph or image of a wall, room, product, design, interior space or similar subject, we may use that image to better understand your requirement and assist with product or design recommendations.

We may use manual review or automated image-analysis technology to assist with initial categorisation, product selection, design suggestions or enquiry handling.

Automated analysis may not always be accurate. Final confirmation of a product, design, article number, colour, measurement, price, material or suitability may require review by Nyasara staff.

Images you provide are not intended to be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate reason or permission.

5. Advertising, Meta Platforms and Enquiry Attribution

Nyasara may advertise through platforms such as Facebook and Instagram and may receive enquiries through Click-to-WhatsApp advertisements or other advertising formats.

Where provided by the relevant platform, Nyasara may receive or retain information such as campaign identifiers, advertisement identifiers, advertisement headlines, referral information, source information or similar attribution data.

We may use this information for customer support, sales operations, lead management, marketing analysis, advertising performance measurement and business reporting.

Your use of Meta, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google or other third-party platforms is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those platforms.

6. Cookies, Website Analytics and Technical Information

Nyasara may use cookies, website analytics, security technologies or similar tools to operate, protect, understand and improve nyasara.com.

These technologies may process technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, referring page, pages visited, website performance, approximate geographic information derived from network data and similar usage information.

Our website is hosted and delivered using Cloudflare and related internet infrastructure. Such providers may process technical information necessary for website delivery, security, performance, caching, abuse prevention and network operation.

If Nyasara introduces additional advertising, analytics, remarketing or tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Service Providers and Technology Partners

Nyasara may use third-party service providers to operate parts of its business and technology infrastructure.

These may include providers of WhatsApp messaging, social media, advertising, cloud hosting, databases, artificial intelligence, website hosting, analytics, customer relationship management, spreadsheets, internal applications, automation, communication systems, logistics and other business software.

Our technology providers may include services operated by Meta, Google, OpenAI, Cloudflare, and other software, cloud, communication, automation or infrastructure providers used by Nyasara from time to time.

These providers may process information on our behalf or as independently governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Where information is processed using infrastructure located outside India, such processing will be handled subject to applicable law and the practices of the relevant service provider.

8. How We Share Information

Nyasara does not sell personal information to advertisers.

We may share information where reasonably necessary with Nyasara employees, authorised sales representatives, service providers, technology partners, installation teams, logistics partners, contractors, professional advisers or other parties involved in fulfilling a customer request or supporting our business operations.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, regulatory authority, government request, or where reasonably necessary to protect Nyasara, our customers, our systems or others from fraud, misuse, security threats or unlawful activity.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably required for the relevant purpose.

9. Marketing, Follow-Ups and Customer Communication

If you contact Nyasara, request a catalogue, ask for pricing, respond to an advertisement, enquire about a product or otherwise express interest in our products or services, we may contact you regarding that enquiry through WhatsApp, telephone or another appropriate communication channel.

Follow-up communication may include relevant product information, pricing, catalogue updates, project assistance, availability information, dealer or wholesale information, or related support.

Where required by law or applicable platform rules, promotional communication will be sent only with the necessary permission or lawful basis.

You may ask Nyasara to stop promotional or non-essential follow-up communication at any time by contacting us or informing our team through the same communication channel.

10. Data Storage and Retention

Nyasara retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Retention may be necessary for managing enquiries, customer follow-ups, orders, quotations, dealer relationships, customer service, installation or delivery coordination, accounting records, tax requirements, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, legal obligations or other legitimate business-record requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted, anonymised, archived or otherwise handled in accordance with our operational and legal requirements.

11. Data Security

Nyasara uses reasonable technical, organisational and operational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration, destruction or disclosure.

These safeguards may include secure cloud infrastructure, access controls, platform security, HTTPS encryption, restricted staff access, authentication controls and other appropriate security measures.

However, no internet transmission, messaging platform, cloud service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the request, you may contact Nyasara to ask us to correct or update inaccurate information, address a privacy-related concern, stop certain promotional communications, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or request deletion of information where applicable and legally permissible.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, tax, contractual, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, regulatory or legitimate business-record purposes.

Requests can be made using the contact information provided at the end of this Privacy Policy.

13. Children's Privacy

Nyasara's products, website, advertisements and services are primarily intended for adults, homeowners, dealers, businesses, contractors, architects, interior designers and other professionals.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for advertising or marketing purposes.

If we become aware that personal information relating to a child has been provided to us in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we may take reasonable steps to address the matter.

14. Third-Party Websites and Platforms

Nyasara's website, WhatsApp communications, advertisements, social-media pages or messages may contain links to third-party websites, applications, social networks or services.

Nyasara does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, security practices, content or terms of those third-party services.

We encourage users to review the relevant privacy policies before providing personal information directly to third-party platforms.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Nyasara may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business operations, products, technology, customer-service systems, advertising practices, legal requirements or data-processing activities.

Any revised Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated Last Updated date.

Material changes may also be communicated through an appropriate channel where required or considered appropriate.
